# English and Arabic keyword strategy

Research date: August 2026. Exact volume and difficulty must be validated in Google Keyword Planner and Royal Maskan Search Console; live-result analysis cannot supply reliable proprietary volume figures.

## Priority English targets

- `UAE villa construction cost calculator` — primary construction-tool intent.
- `villa construction cost UAE` and `cost to build a villa in UAE` — supporting phrases.
- `UAE villa renovation cost calculator` — primary renovation-tool intent.
- `villa renovation cost UAE` and `villa renovation cost per sqm UAE` — supporting phrases.
- `Abu Dhabi office fit-out cost calculator` — primary fit-out intent and tighter geographic relevance.
- `office fit-out cost Abu Dhabi` and `fit out cost per sqm Abu Dhabi` — supporting phrases.

## Priority Arabic targets

- `حاسبة تكلفة بناء فيلا في الإمارات` — primary construction page.
- `تكلفة بناء فيلا في الإمارات` and `سعر متر البناء في الإمارات` — supporting construction phrases.
- `حاسبة تكلفة تجديد وترميم فيلا` — primary renovation page.
- `تكلفة تجديد فيلا في الإمارات` and `تكلفة ترميم فيلا` — supporting renovation phrases.
- `حاسبة تكلفة تشطيب مكتب في أبوظبي` — primary fit-out page.
- `تكلفة تشطيب مكتب في أبوظبي` and `سعر تشطيب المتر في أبوظبي` — supporting fit-out phrases.

Arabic calculator-specific results were visibly less crowded than broad English renovation queries during research. Each page therefore targets one natural, high-intent Arabic phrase rather than repeating a large keyword list.
